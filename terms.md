# Evaluation terms — Agent Readiness Gate

Updated: 6 November 2026. Maintainer: Maciej Rubczyński. Contact: **rubczynski.maciej@gmail.com**.

The current app is an evaluation prototype intended for development/testing with synthetic evidence. It is not offered as a production safety, compliance or release-enforcement service. There is no app subscription fee for this evaluation; Atlassian products and platform usage remain subject to their own terms and charges.

Install only on a site you are authorized to administer. Follow your organization's Jira access rules and Atlassian's applicable terms. Do not enter credentials, sensitive personal information, or proprietary customer evidence into this evaluation app.

The app assesses supplied evidence under a deterministic policy. READY is not a guarantee of safety, correctness, regulatory compliance or permission to deploy. It does not block Jira transitions. The user remains responsible for independently reviewing evidence and release decisions.

Approval is an explicit action by an authorized reviewer. Creating a remediation issue is a separate, confirmed Jira write and does not approve the release. The Rovo advisor provides read-only explanations; it cannot change readiness or invent approval.

Features may change during evaluation. No availability or support response-time commitment is offered. Review the [privacy notice](privacy.md), particularly current retention/deletion limitations, before using the app. Contact the maintainer with questions or problems. These evaluation materials do not replace any mandatory rights or the agreements governing your Atlassian account.
